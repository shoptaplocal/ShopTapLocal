FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything customers need to know.

Do customers need an app?

No. Most modern NFC-compatible smartphones can open the programmed link directly.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

It works with most NFC-compatible iPhone and Android devices. A QR backup is included.

Can TapLocal use my logo and colors?

Yes. Basic custom branding is included with each product and bundle.

What can the product open?

Review pages, websites, menus, digital contact cards, social profiles, payment links, booking pages, and more.

Can I order for several locations?

Yes. Choose the Multi-Location Bundle or request a custom quote for a larger order.

Can the destination be changed later?

Some setups can be reprogrammed. Contact TapLocal before ordering if future updates are important to your use case.
