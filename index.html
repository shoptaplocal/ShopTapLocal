FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything customers need to know.

Do customers need an app?

No. Most modern smartphones can open the programmed link directly by tapping the product.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

It works with most NFC-compatible iPhone and Android devices. A QR backup is included for added convenience.

Can TapLocal use my logo and colors?

Yes. Basic custom branding is included with each product and bundle.

What can the product open?

Google reviews, websites, menus, digital contact cards, social profiles, payment links, booking pages, and more.

Can I order for several locations?

Yes. Choose the Multi-Location Bundle or request a custom quote for larger orders.
